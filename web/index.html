What this study did and did not do

Did

  • Pulled public records from 11 sources: US Labor, USAspending, SEC EDGAR, the UK, Canadian and Australian contract registries, OpenAlex, Wikipedia, Wikidata and firm sitemaps.
  • Kept raw extracts untouched; every transformation is a script (src/clean.py).
  • Defined every metric with numerator, denominator, filters and missing-data rule (outputs/tables/metrics.csv).
  • Registered every written claim with its source and limitation, and tested it against the data on every run (claims.csv, pytest).
  • Used one strict name matcher so "Bain & Schindele" or "McKinsey Fencing" cannot leak in.

Did not

  • Compare revenue across the decade: only BCG reports it.
  • Measure industry exposure, client lists or strategy: none of it is public in a comparable form.
  • Run significance tests or causal analysis; all findings are descriptive.
  • Use confidential, paywalled or scraped-behind-login data. Only sitemaps named in robots.txt were read; no pages were crawled.
  • Verify most McKinsey and Bain scale figures against their original pages (see the verification table).
  • Build the Power BI dashboard or slide deck from the original brief; this site and the written findings replace them.
